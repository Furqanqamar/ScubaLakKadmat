Scuba Lak original media update — 25 October 2026

Reviewed all 18 supplied files: 10 photographs and 8 short films.
Subjects: lagoon training and mask fitting; shore briefings; equipment preparation;
boat departures; Kadmat lagoon and sunsets; the dive centre; Agatti and Amini.
Captions describe the visible scenes. A filename referring to a young diver has
been captioned as one-to-one training without making an age claim.

Placement
- Gallery: all 18 originals, with subject and photo/film filters.
- Home: four original photo teasers.
- About: actual dive-centre photograph and a three-item photo/film story.
- Kadmat: real lagoon imagery and three island films.
- Lakshadweep: actual Agatti and Amini photographs.
- Experiences: original lagoon/course images and contextual media stories.
- Beginner course pages: original photographs and training moments.

Delivery
- Photographs: colour-managed sRGB WebP, high quality, up to 2560px on the long
  edge, with 480px and 800px responsive alternatives and tiny inline previews.
- Films: upright H.264/AAC MP4, SDR tone-mapped from iPhone HLG originals,
  1080px desktop and 720px mobile widths at 30fps, fast-start metadata.
- Film excerpts: 8–16 seconds. Original audio is retained; playback begins muted
  and visitors can enable sound with the native controls.
- All delivery variants together: 129.2MB; original supplied files: 688.1MB.
  This is the complete collection, not the initial page download.
- Films are absent from the page DOM until opened. Cards load only still posters.
- Lazy images, explicit dimensions, loading/error states and reduced-motion support.
- Original photographs, existing team assets and the original hero film are not edited.
  Compressed delivery copies are visually checked; compression is not lossless.

Caching
- All new delivery filenames contain a content fingerprint.
- Production preview uses one-year immutable caching for these files, with byte
  ranges for MP4 and cache revalidation for unversioned assets.
- A small service worker caches only requested fingerprinted original images,
  bounded to 100 entries. It does not prefetch, intercept HTML or handle videos.
- Videos use normal browser/HTTP caching. GitHub Pages controls its own HTTP
  cache lifetime; application code cannot override that host's response headers.

Maintenance
- Captions and categories: scripts/original-media.json.
- Repeatable conversion: scripts/import-original-media.mjs and the macOS
  ImageIO helper scripts/convert-original-photos.swift.
- Shared viewer, media collection and story components; copy and styles are
  separated in media-copy.ts and styles/media.css.
- Generation outputs are committed so CI doesn't require the private originals.

Validation
- Angular production build and prerendering of 37 routes.
- SEO checks for 36 indexable pages.
- Asset fingerprints, image placeholders and variant integrity checks.
- Browser checks: responsive grid, filters, on-demand mobile playback, modal
  closing, no horizontal overflow at mobile and desktop sizes.
