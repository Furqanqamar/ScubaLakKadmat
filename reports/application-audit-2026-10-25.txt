Limitations and remaining work
- Delivery provider/endpoint and request/acknowledgement contract need approval.
  The documented {accepted:true} contract is a proposed adapter boundary, not
  an assertion that an approved backend exists. Configure its CORS, validation,
  abuse protection and real delivery monitoring separately. No live enquiry
  was sent during testing. Real inbox delivery is not tested or enabled.
- Missing names/roles, approved biographies/history/statistics/testimonials and
  actual accommodation/transfer/inclusion/cancellation/weather terms remain
  pending. The app avoids presenting them as confirmed facts.
- Physical iOS/Android, software-keyboard behavior, real touch swipes, screen
  readers and constrained-network/device performance need device testing.
  Swipe and remaining reduced-motion branches were source inspected; OS motion
  and connection emulation were not available for a full browser test.
- The latest always-playing hero request removes the manual pause mechanism.
  Continuous decorative motion without a pause option is an accessibility
  trade-off; do not claim full WCAG motion compliance. Browser autoplay and
  power-saving restrictions can still prevent playback. Mobile now downloads
  a video automatically, unlike the earlier opt-in revision.
- No new Lighthouse score or Core Web Vitals field result is claimed. Video
  size and functional browser checks do not establish a guaranteed score.
- Publication, custom domains, analytics and external business listings were
  not changed in this application-code task. Search ranking is not guaranteed.
