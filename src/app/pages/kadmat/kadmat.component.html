Plan by geography

The best Kadmat itinerary leaves room for the tide.

Most experiences cluster around the western lagoon and reef edge, while the island’s length gives you space to move between water time, village life and unhurried shore walks. Exact dive and boat locations are chosen daily by the team according to sea state, visibility and reef conditions.
