Culture, not costume

Come curious.
Listen closely.

Lakshadweep’s culture is shaped by island geography, Islam, seafaring, fishing, coconut, boat-building, food, language and deep community ties. Guests are welcomed into a living place, not a themed experience. Dress thoughtfully, ask before photographing people, respect prayer spaces and let the reef stay wild.
